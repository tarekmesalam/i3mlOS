# Security Policy

i3mlOS is pre-alpha. Until a formal process exists, report vulnerabilities
privately to tarekmesalam25@gmail.com. Do not open public issues for security
reports.

Two standing rules from the project's claims ladder:

- We do not use the word **"secure"** anywhere until an external audit has
  happened. What we claim before that is behavior CI can prove.
- "Zero third-party code in the image" means no inherited dependency CVEs —
  it does **not** mean bug-free. Our own bugs have no advisory feed except
  this policy and the fuzzers.
